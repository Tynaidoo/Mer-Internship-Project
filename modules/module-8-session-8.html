Essential Workplace Policies and Professional Responsibilities

Understand essential workplace policies, including information protection, safety, grievance procedures, responsible whistleblowing, cybersecurity and professional ethics.

Session 8 · 1 hour

Purpose & learning objectives

Understand essential workplace policies, including information protection, safety, grievance procedures, responsible whistleblowing, cybersecurity and professional ethics.

Learning objectives

By the end of this session, you should be able to:

  1. Explain the basic purpose of POPIA.
  2. Identify hazards and how to report unsafe conditions.
  3. Describe grievance and whistleblowing procedures.
  4. Understand cybersecurity and professional conduct obligations.
  5. Choose appropriate policies or reporting channels.

Time allocation: 1 hour

SPARK · Opening discussion

Think before you begin

An intern wants to report suspected fraud by a supervisor. Another believes a supervisor treated them unfairly and wants to lodge a complaint. Which workplace policy or procedure applies to each?

Grievance procedures

A grievance procedure is a structured way to raise workplace concerns. Review the applicable policy; approach an appropriate supervisor or representative where safe; submit a formal written grievance when necessary; record facts, dates and supporting evidence; and follow the prescribed process. Use alternative channels where the supervisor is implicated or the concern involves harassment, threats or serious misconduct. Internal processes do not automatically suspend external legal deadlines.

Whistleblowing and reporting wrongdoing

Report suspected wrongdoing through authorised channels, distinguish facts from assumptions, preserve evidence lawfully, avoid gossip or unauthorised investigations and seek guidance from HR, a mentor or legal adviser. The Protected Disclosures Act may provide protection in qualifying circumstances, depending on legal requirements.

Confidentiality, cybersecurity and acceptable use

Policies may cover passwords, multi-factor authentication, company email and devices, cloud-sharing permissions, software downloads, AI tools, social media and reporting phishing. Do not share organisational information externally merely because you can access it or helped create it. Follow POPIA and data-breach procedures when personal information is exposed.

Other policies to know

Understand leave and attendance, performance management, discipline, grievance, conflicts of interest, gifts and anti-corruption, records management, remote work, IT use, harassment and discrimination. Request access to the policies relevant to your placement during onboarding.
