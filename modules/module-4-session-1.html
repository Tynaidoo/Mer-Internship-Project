Module 4 • Session 1

Identifying and Addressing Your Learning Fears

Identify fears around learning and online learning, understand how they affect behaviour, and develop practical strategies for responding constructively.

Learning Confidence • Self-Regulation • Help-Seeking

Purpose of Session

This session is focused on identifying your fears around learning and online learning. You will identify strategies for managing your fears and have an opportunity to share them with others.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this session, you should be able to:

  • Identify common fears and anxieties associated with learning.
  • Recognise how learning fears influence your behaviour and learning.
  • Apply practical strategies for managing learning fears and responding constructively to challenges.

What does this tell you about learning?

What Are Learning Fears?

Learning fears refer to the anxiety, apprehension, or worry that people experience when they face a new learning challenge or task. These fears can manifest in different ways, such as fear of failure, fear of looking foolish, fear of the unknown, fear of making mistakes, or fear of being judged.

It is important to understand that learning fears are a natural part of the learning process and that everyone experiences them at some point. It is not uncommon for interns to feel nervous, anxious, or even overwhelmed when confronted with a task. Learning fears can be a sign that you are challenging yourself and stepping outside of your comfort zone.

Activity 4.1.2: From Learning Fear to Action

Apply the strategies you have learned to realistic situations you may encounter during your internship.

Scenario

Thando is a new intern working on a research project. His supervisor asks him to use a software programme he has never used before to analyse some information and prepare a short presentation for the team.

Thando feels anxious because he has never used the software. He thinks:

“Everyone else probably knows how to use this. If I ask for help, my supervisor might think I am not capable.”

He opens the programme but becomes overwhelmed by the number of features. Instead of asking for help, he decides to work on another task and tells himself that he will come back to it later. The deadline gets closer. Thando becomes even more anxious because he has made little progress.
