21st-Century Skills & Employability Competencies

Explore the transferable and technical skills that support employability in changing workplaces, assess your current capabilities and identify practical development priorities.

Employability • Transferable Skills • AI Fluency • Career Development

Purpose

To introduce interns to the broad transferable skills that allow people to perform effectively in changing workplaces, beyond technical knowledge alone, and provide practical frameworks to self-assess and upgrade their skills.
Time allocation: 45–60 minutes

Learning outcomes

Interns should be able to:

  • Define employability/transferable skills.
  • Identify key 21st-century skills.
  • Recognise the difference between technical and transferable skills.
  • Identify their own strengths and development areas.
  • Explain why skills need to continue developing throughout a career.
Spark

If an employer could only evaluate you on five abilities—not your degree, marks or technical knowledge—which five abilities would you want them to see?

Skills

Workplace capability includes both technical knowledge and broader human or transferable skills. Technical skills relate to specific tools, knowledge or tasks, while transferable skills help you work effectively with people and respond to different situations. Some capabilities can sit across both categories.

Skills Evidence & AI-Powered Gap Analysis

A useful skill statement must be supported by evidence rather than vague self-ratings. A simple evidence structure to use on CVs, interviews, and performance reviews is SARL.

📍 SituationSet the scene and describe the challenge.
🎬 ActionExplain the specific steps you took.
🏆 ResultDescribe the measurable outcome or impact.
🪴 LearningIdentify the core skill or lesson gained.

Furthermore, Generative AI can serve as a personalised career coaching assistant. Interns can utilise Large Language Models (LLMs) to analyse job descriptions, map required competencies against their current skill sets, and generate targeted skill-building roadmaps.

Effective Skill-Mapping Prompting Framework

Context

Specify your target role/industry and current background.

Action

Ask AI to identify top technical and soft skill gaps.

Output

Request a 4-week actionable learning roadmap with specific resources.

Activity: The AI Gap-Bridge Challenge

Step 1

Draft Your SARL Evidence Statement

Write out a single past experience using the SARL framework.

Step 2

Run the Gap-Analysis Prompt

Copy and paste your SARL statement alongside a target job description into your AI tool, using the prompt structure below:

Context: I am an intern targeting a role as a [Insert Target Job Title, e.g., Junior Data Analyst] in the [Insert Industry] industry. Here is a recent SARL evidence statement from my experience: [Paste your SARL text].

Action: Analyse this job description: [Paste a target job description]. Compare it against my SARL statement to identify my top 3 technical and soft skill gaps.

Output: Provide a 4-week, step-by-step learning roadmap with specific free resources (e.g., courses, documentation, projects) to help me bridge these exact skill gaps.

Discussion Forum Share

Submit your generated 4-week roadmap alongside your initial SARL statement in the course forum. Read through one classmate’s submission and leave a tip on how they can further strengthen their prompt.

Checklist
