Module 6 · Session 1

Developing Your Internship ePortfolio

Begin documenting your workplace learning, evidence, reflection and professional development throughout your internship.

ePortfolio • Evidence • Reflection • Professional Development

Purpose of Session

The purpose of this session is to introduce you to the Internship ePortfolio and help you begin documenting your workplace learning and professional development. Your ePortfolio is a living record of your internship journey that you will continue to develop throughout the programme.

Learning Outcomes

  • Define an ePortfolio and explain its purpose within your internship.
  • Identify appropriate content and evidence to include.
  • Explain the relationship between experience, evidence and reflection.
  • Create the basic structure of your Internship ePortfolio.
  • Develop a short professional introduction.
Spark

If You Had to Show Someone What You Can Do...

Imagine that at the end of your internship someone asks, “What did you actually learn and achieve during your internship?” Think about what you did, the skill you used, what evidence could demonstrate it, and what you learned.

What Is an ePortfolio?

An ePortfolio is a digital collection of information and evidence that demonstrates your experiences, learning, skills, achievements and development. Unlike a CV, it allows you to demonstrate skills through evidence and reflection.

Example: Instead of only saying “I have good presentation skills,” include the experience of presenting research findings, evidence such as slides or supervisor feedback, and a reflection on what you learned.

Why Are You Creating One?

  • Document workplace experiences and skills.
  • Collect evidence of your work and record feedback.
  • Reflect on learning and recognise strengths.
  • Identify areas for development and track internship goals.
  • Build a record of professional development.

Activity 1.1

What Would You Include?

Imagine you are three months into your internship. Consider team meetings, project research, supervisor feedback, presentations, workplace training, collaborative problem-solving and learning a new digital tool. For each experience you select, identify the experience, skill demonstrated, possible evidence and what you learned.

Evidence: Show, Don't Just Tell

Evidence might include presentations, reports, research outputs, project plans, appropriate photographs, certificates, supervisor or mentor feedback, designs, meeting contributions and completed learning activities. Always consider workplace confidentiality before uploading material.

Activity 1.2

Evidence or Just a Claim?

Which provides stronger evidence: “I am a good communicator,” or “I presented our project findings during a team meeting and received feedback from my supervisor on the clarity of my presentation”?

Rewrite “I am good at working in teams” so it refers to a specific experience or evidence.

Building Your Internship ePortfolio

A basic structure could include:

  1. Home
  2. About Me
  3. My Internship
  4. My Goals
  5. Projects and Experiences
  6. Skills Development
  7. Reflections
  8. Achievements

Activity 1.3

Create Your Internship ePortfolio

Create the eight sections above using the platform identified for your internship programme. Focus on creating the structure; evidence and reflections will be added as your internship progresses.

Your Professional Introduction

Your About Me section should communicate who you are, your educational or training background, interests, existing strengths or skills, development areas and professional aspirations. Keep it clear, authentic and concise.

Activity 1.4

Develop Your Professional Introduction

Consider your background, interests, strengths, development goals and future aspirations. Then write an approximately 80–120 word professional introduction.

Checklist & Reflection

  • ✓ I can explain what an ePortfolio is and why I will use one.
  • ✓ I can distinguish a skill claim from evidence.
  • ✓ I understand experience, evidence, reflection and development.
  • ✓ I have created the basic structure of my Internship ePortfolio.
  • ✓ I have created my About Me section.
  • ✓ I understand that confidential workplace information should not be shared.

Reflection

Complete: “By the end of my internship, I would like my ePortfolio to show that I have...”

Identify one skill to develop, one experience to gain and one achievement you would be proud to include.
