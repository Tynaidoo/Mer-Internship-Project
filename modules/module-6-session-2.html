Module 6 · Session 2

Developing Your LinkedIn Profile

Create a professional LinkedIn profile that represents your education, internship experience, skills, interests and developing professional identity.

LinkedIn • Professional Identity • Skills • Career Development

Purpose of Session

The purpose of this session is to help you create and develop a professional LinkedIn profile that represents your education, internship experience, skills, interests and developing professional identity. Your profile can grow with you throughout your internship and future career.

Learning Outcomes

  • Explain the purpose of LinkedIn as a professional networking platform.
  • Identify the key components of an effective LinkedIn profile.
  • Develop an appropriate professional headline.
  • Write an effective About section.
  • Present your education, internship experience and skills professionally.
Spark

What Does Your Digital Profile Say About You?

Imagine someone from an organisation you would like to work for finds your LinkedIn profile and spends 30 seconds looking at it. What three things would you want them to learn about you?

What Is LinkedIn?

LinkedIn is a professional networking platform that allows individuals to create a digital professional profile, connect with others, follow organisations, explore career opportunities and communicate their experience, education and skills.

LinkedIn vs CV: A CV is normally adapted for a particular opportunity. Your LinkedIn profile is a continuously developing professional presence that grows as your education, skills and workplace experience develop.

Activity 2.1

What Does Your Profile Communicate?

  1. What does my current profile say about me?
  2. Is it clear what I am currently doing?
  3. Does it communicate my interests?
  4. Does it demonstrate any of my skills?
  5. Is important information missing?
  6. What is the first thing I would improve?

Developing Your Professional Headline

Activity 2.2

Develop Your Professional Headline

Option 1: Focus on your current role and area of interest.

Option 2: Focus on your current role, interests and developing skills.

Choose the headline that is accurate, professional and best reflects the direction in which you want to develop.

Developing Your About Section

As an intern, focus on your educational background, current internship, professional interests, strengths, skills you are developing, what motivates you and where you would like to develop professionally.

Example: I am a recent graduate currently gaining practical workplace experience through an internship. I am interested in research, digital technologies and how organisations use information to solve problems. Through my internship, I am developing my research, communication and project management skills while gaining experience working in a professional environment.

Activity 2.3

Develop Your About Section

Consider: Who am I? What am I doing now? What interests me? What can I contribute? What am I developing? Where am I going?

Activity 2.4

Build and Review Your LinkedIn Profile

  1. Check your name, profile photograph, headline and location.
  2. Add or improve your About section.
  3. Ensure your education information is accurate.
  4. Add your current internship and describe what you are doing or learning.
  5. Add relevant skills based on actual experiences.
  6. Review the full profile for clarity and professionalism.

LinkedIn Profile Checklist

  • ✓ I can explain the purpose of LinkedIn.
  • ✓ I have an appropriate profile photograph.
  • ✓ I have developed a professional headline.
  • ✓ I have written or improved my About section.
  • ✓ My education information is accurate.
  • ✓ I have added my internship to my Experience section.
  • ✓ I have identified relevant skills based on actual experiences.
  • ✓ My profile accurately represents my current level of experience.
  • ✓ I have reviewed my profile for spelling, grammar and professionalism.

Reflection

What would you like to add to your LinkedIn profile by the end of your internship that you cannot add today? Identify one skill, one achievement, one project or experience and one area of professional growth. Save your response in your Internship ePortfolio.
