Career Ownership & Professional Identity

Take active ownership of your career trajectory, professional identity, reputation, digital presence and workplace relationships.

Career Ownership • Personal Brand • Digital Footprint • Networking

Purpose

To empower interns to take active ownership of their career trajectory, define their professional identity, build authentic relationships, manage their digital presence, and construct a compelling personal brand.
Time allocation: 45–60 minutes

Learning outcomes

Interns should be able to:

  • Define explicit learning objectives, experiences, and success criteria for the internship.
  • Identify behaviours that build a positive, reliable professional reputation.
  • Develop an authentic personal brand and manage digital footprint/presence responsibly.
  • Develop strategic networking and relationship-building plans across organisational tiers.
Spark

When someone searches your name online or asks a colleague, “What is [Your Name] known for?”, what story would you like your digital and professional footprint to tell?

Career Ownership, Personal Branding & Digital Footprint

Career Ownership

Actively shaping your learning, testing interests, collecting evidence of strengths, and understanding work environments rather than waiting for a career path to appear.

Personal Brand

Your reputation, work ethic, and how people describe you when you are not in the room.

Digital Footprint

Public profiles, portfolios, GitHub, posts, and comments. Before posting, consider audience, permanence, context, and professional alignment.

Daily Work Brand

Being known as reliable, detail-oriented, positive, or a fast learner.

Unique Value Proposition (UVP)

The intersection of your skills, interests, and unique strengths.

Activity: Own Your Career & Build Your Personal Brand

Instructions: Reflect on how you currently present yourself as a professional. Complete the questions below to create a simple personal brand snapshot.

1. Career Ownership

Identify one action you can take in the next month to actively explore your interests, develop a skill, or learn more about a work environment.

2. Your Work Brand

Imagine a colleague is asked: “What is this person like to work with?”

Write three words or qualities you would like them to use to describe you.

Now identify one piece of evidence that demonstrates one of these qualities.

3. Your Digital Footprint

Before posting something publicly, what is one question you should ask yourself to make sure the post supports your professional brand?

4. Your Unique Value Proposition (UVP)

“I bring together my interest in ________, my strength in ________, and my skill in ________ to add value by ________.”

Final Reflection

What is one thing you will do differently from now on to take greater ownership of your career or strengthen your personal brand?

Strategic Networking & Relationship Building

Networking is not exchanging business cards; it is building authentic, mutually beneficial professional relationships.

  • Reputation is Built Daily: Through reliability, quality of delivery, communication, curiosity, and how you treat people.
  • Authentic Networking: Networking is not transactional self-promotion or collecting contacts; it is building genuine professional relationships through shared interests, helpfulness, and follow-up.
  • Coffee Chats & Informational Interviews: Respectfully requesting 15-minute syncs with colleagues in different departments.
  • Mentors vs. Sponsors: Mentors offer advice; sponsors actively advocate for you behind closed doors.
  • Setting Internship Goals: Establishing clear 30-60-90 day personal development benchmarks.

Formula for Networking

📝 PrepareKnow why you want to connect.
🤝 ConnectStart an authentic conversation.
💡 ContributeAdd value and show genuine interest.
📩 Follow upMaintain the relationship.

30–60–90 Day Development Benchmarks

30 Days

What do you want to learn, understand or establish?

60 Days

What capability or relationship do you want to develop?

90 Days

What evidence of growth or contribution do you want to demonstrate?

Activity: Informational Interview Outreach Workshop

Instructions: Draft a personalised, approximately 100-word outreach email to a senior professional or manager in another team at your company requesting a 15-minute virtual coffee chat.

Ensure it includes:

  1. Warm connection
  2. Specific reason for reaching out
  3. Clear, low-friction ask
